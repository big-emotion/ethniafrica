# Audience audit — 2026-09-07

Period measured: last 30 days, to 2026-09-07, from the self-hosted Plausible at
`stats.ethniafrica.com`.

**These are consented sessions only.** Plausible loads after the consent banner
is accepted, so every figure below is a floor, not the audience. At this volume
one visitor's afternoon moves a page several ranks — rank and ratio are the
signal, absolute counts are not.

This is the **baseline run**. There is no previous report to compare against.

**LinkedIn carries nearly half the site's traffic.** The launch plan schedules
LinkedIn for Phase 3, from 2 November, as one post per Monday — the last channel
to be invested and the first to deliver.

**TikTok, Instagram and YouTube appear nowhere.** Three Shorts were published to
all three on 2026-09-05, two days before this measurement: Afrique, Lingala and
the Nigeria Codex cut. They contributed no attributed visit.

Before concluding those channels do not convert, note that **no outbound social
link is UTM-tagged**. Untagged referrers from in-app browsers land in
`Direct / None`, which is the second-largest bucket here and has the _best_
engagement of any source (34 % bounce, 6m47). The video-to-site link is
currently unmeasured, not disproved. Tagging is a prerequisite to any decision
about the channel mix.

Nine visitors in ten are on mobile, and they bounce **3.6× more often** than
desktop visitors while staying **one sixth** as long. The site is mobile-first by
charter; measured behaviour says the mobile experience is where the audience is
and where it is being lost. This is the largest single gap in the report.

The desktop figures rest on five visitors and should not be quoted as precision.
The direction of the gap is nonetheless unambiguous.

### The dead-end pattern is concentrated on peuple fiches

Six pages combine a 100 % or near-100 % bounce with high scroll depth and minutes
of attention. **Five of the six are peuple fiches.** These readers did not fail
to engage — they finished. The fiche then offered nowhere to go: no sibling
people, no parent family, no country, no next question. This is a template
problem, and one fix reaches all 776 peuple fiches.

## Findings

1. The site's second-most-visited page, `/fr/jeux/mercator`, is a dead end: 15 visitors, 92 % bounce, 76 % scroll depth. It earns attention and terminates the session.
2. Peuple fiches read to completion end the session. Five of six high-scroll pages with ~100 % bounce are peuple fiches — a template gap, not a content gap.
3. LinkedIn delivers 45 % of traffic while scheduled last in the publishing plan; the three video channels deliver no attributed traffic at all.
4. No outbound social link carries UTM parameters, so finding 3 cannot yet distinguish "the videos do not convert" from "the conversions are filed as Direct".
5. 91 % of visitors are on mobile, where they bounce 3.6× more than on desktop and stay one sixth as long.
6. 97 % of the corpus received no visit. Patronymes are the largest fiche family (796) and the least discovered (1.1 %).
7. Hubs work. `/fr/atlas/pays`, `/peuples`, `/langues` and `/recherche` all record 0 % bounce. Whatever fails, it is not the directory layer.

## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- **Priority 1 — the fiche next step.** Findings 1 and 2. Design the end-of-page continuation for the peuple fiche template, and for the Mercator game. Reach: 776 fiches plus the site's second page. Falsifier: bounce on peuple fiches and on `/fr/jeux/mercator` in the next audit.
- **Priority 2 — the mobile gap.** Finding 5. Diagnose at 430px why mobile sessions end at 2m10 against desktop's 12m12. Falsifier: mobile bounce, mobile visit duration.
- **Priority 3 — bounce-through on `FLG_KROU`, `PPL_LUBA_KASAI2`, `PPL_BARIBA`.** Low time, low scroll, total bounce: the promise made upstream does not match the page. Check what links to them.
- **Instrumentation, not design:** finding 4. UTM tagging on every outbound social link. Hand back rather than designing around it.

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- **Do not schedule new subjects before finding 4 is fixed.** Three videos shipped and the report cannot say what they did. Tagging first.
- **LinkedIn is the measured channel.** Finding 3 contradicts the plan's phasing. Reweight before Phase 3, and bring the ledger's numbers when you do.
- **The Mercator game is the audience's actual entry point after the home page.** Finding 1. There is proven demand for the true-size-of-Africa subject; the corpus and the social pillars should both exploit it — `La carte cachée` is the pillar that already fits.
- **Countries are discovered, patronymes are not** (finding 6). 796 patronyme fiches at 1.1 % coverage is the largest unexploited asset on the site, and `Le vrai nom` is the pillar built for it.
- **Do not answer finding 2 with more content.** Converting a dead end is a template change, not a publication.
