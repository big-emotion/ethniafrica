# Audience audit — 2026-09-12

Period measured: Plausible's `30d` window, 2026-08-13 to 2026-09-11, from the
self-hosted instance at `stats.ethniafrica.com`. Movement is read on two
non-overlapping windows: **2026-09-04 → 09-06** (the previous report's data) and
**2026-09-07 → 09-12** (everything since, today partial).

**These are consented sessions only.** Plausible loads after the consent banner
is accepted, so every figure below is a floor, not the audience. At this volume
one visitor's afternoon moves a page several ranks — rank and ratio are the
signal, absolute counts are not.

## Two corrections to read before any number

**1. The 2026-09-07 baseline was three days, not thirty.** Plausible's daily
graph is zero on every day before 2026-09-04. The previous report's "last 30
days" therefore measured 2026-09-04 → 09-06, a window built around a launch
spike (35 visitors on 09-05, the day five Shorts and the launch post shipped).
This run's `30d` window is in fact eight days of data. Every "30-day" figure in
both reports is a launch-week figure.

| Day      | 09-04 | 09-05 | 09-06 | 09-07 | 09-08 | 09-09 | 09-10 | 09-11 | 09-12 (partial) |
| -------- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | --------------- |
| Visitors | 2     | 35    | 19    | 12    | 11    | 14    | 6     | 4     | 9               |

**2. Bounce rate stopped being comparable on 2026-09-07 at 20:27 UTC.** Release
v4.6.0 shipped `trackEvent` (#930), whose custom events are sent as interactive.
`fiche:depth` fires on scroll, so a visitor who reads one page and leaves is no
longer counted as a bounce. The proof is in one row: Google visits since 09-07
record **41 % bounce at exactly 1.00 views per visit** (22 visits, 22
pageviews). A bounce rate that falls after that date is an instrumentation
change, not a behaviour change. This report uses **views per visit and exit
rate** as the comparable measures, and every bounce figure after 09-07 carries
that caveat.

## Findings

1. Plausible recorded zero visitors before 2026-09-04, so the 2026-09-07 "30-day" baseline was three launch days (35 visitors on 09-05), and this run's `30d` window is eight days.
2. Bounce rate is not comparable across 2026-09-07 20:27 UTC: interactive `fiche:depth` events suppress it, visible in Google visits at 41 % bounce and 1.00 views per visit.
3. Daily traffic halved as the launch decayed: 18.3 visitors/day (09-04 → 06) to 9.0/day (09-07 → 12), with views per visit stable at 3.08 → 3.20.
4. LinkedIn fell from 8.3 to 1.5 visitors/day; its 45 % share in the previous report was one post.
5. Google held a flat 3.7 visitors/day, became the largest source (41 % since 09-07), lands on deep fiches (27 of 31 entries), and every Google visit since 09-07 saw exactly one page.
6. The « Poursuivre » chapter recorded zero `fiche:related_click` across 22 visits entering on peuple fiches, whose views per visit fell from 4.33 to 1.05.
7. The dead-end pattern survived the fix: five peuple fiches visited after it (`TEKE`, `VILI`, `MANIANGA`, `ABIDJI`, `ANUAK`) scrolled 76–100 % and exited at 100 %.
8. Mercator's second rank was one LinkedIn post — 15 of its 16 entries, 86.7 % exit — and it fell to 5 visitors; `game:complete` recorded 0 against 3 `game:start` visitors.
9. Search became the second page (6 → 15 visitors, 11.5 % exit rate), while `search:submit` counts only 2 visitors in the window.
10. On measures the instrumentation change does not touch, mobile still underperforms desktop: 2.71 against 4.41 views per visit, 2m35 against 8m08.
11. YouTube and Instagram carry one attributed visit each, TikTok and Facebook none; the redirect that stripped UTM parameters was fixed in production only on 2026-09-12, so video attribution is still unmeasured.
12. The audience moved with the channel: France rose from 36 % to 61 % of visitors and Côte d'Ivoire fell from 22 % to 6 % between the two windows.
13. Only 397 of 796 patronyme fiches are in the sitemap, so half the least-discovered fiche family cannot be found by search.
14. `/fr/dossier` answers 404 and received a visit, and no `page:not_found` event was recorded for any visit since tracking began.

## What could not be measured

- **Unconsented traffic.** By construction. Every figure is a floor.
- **Search Console.** Plausible's Google search-terms breakdown answers `not_configured`: no Search Console property is linked to the instance, and none is reachable without authentication. Impressions, queries and click-through rate for the fiche titles shipped in #930 are therefore unknown.
- **Per-post platform performance** (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn views and retention). This requires authenticated platform access and belongs to `/ethniafrica-content-strategist`. The only view counts cited here come from the publication record.
- **Video-to-site conversion.** UTM parameters were stripped on deep-link redirects until 2026-09-12 (#961).
- **Internal traffic.** Whether the team's own sessions are excluded could not be verified; the baseline's 3.4 visits per desktop visitor suggests they are not.
- **Bounce as a behaviour measure after 2026-09-07.** Replaced by views per visit and exit rate (§2).
- **Anything before 2026-09-04.** Plausible holds no data.

## Method

Plausible's unauthenticated internal API at
`stats.ethniafrica.com/api/stats/ethniafrica.com/`: `top-stats`, `pages`,
`entry-pages`, `exit-pages`, `sources`, `screen-sizes`, `countries`,
`conversions`, `utm_sources`, `utm_campaigns` and `main-graph`. Breakdowns used
`detailed=true` and `limit=300`. The two comparison windows used
`period=custom`. Per-source, per-device and per-event figures used the
`filters=[["is","visit:source",[…]]]`, `visit:device`, `event:name` and
`["contains","visit:entry_page",["/fr/atlas/peuples/PPL_"]]` forms. Re-measuring
2026-08-08 → 09-06 reproduces the previous report's headline exactly (55 / 71 /
219), which is how finding 1 was established.

Corpus counts from `find dataset/source/afrik -name '*.json'`; indexable counts
from production's `sitemap.xml`; release timing from the GitHub releases
(v4.6.0 2026-09-07 20:27 UTC, v4.7.0 2026-09-12 11:11 UTC, v4.8.0 2026-09-12
17:54 UTC). Event emission was checked in source, and the onward chapter's
presence in the live HTML of `/fr/atlas/peuples/PPL_ALUR`.
