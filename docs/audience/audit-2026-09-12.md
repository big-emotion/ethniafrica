# Audience audit — 2026-09-12

Period measured: Plausible's `30d` window, 2026-08-13 → 2026-09-11, from the
self-hosted instance at `stats.ethniafrica.com`.

**These are consented sessions only.** Plausible loads after the consent banner
is accepted, so every figure below is a floor, not the audience. At this volume
one visitor's afternoon moves a page several ranks — rank and ratio are the
signal, absolute counts are not.

## Read this first: the window is 8 days, not 30

The collector recorded **zero visitors on every day before 2026-09-04**, the day
the Plausible CSP fix shipped. Daily unique visitors since:

| 09-04 | 09-05 | 09-06 | 09-07 | 09-08 | 09-09 | 09-10 | 09-11 |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 2     | 35    | 19    | 12    | 11    | 14    | 6     | 4     |

Two consequences:

- **The 2026-09-07 baseline measured 3 days, not 30.** Re-querying its window
  (2026-08-08 → 2026-09-06) returns its figures exactly — 55 visitors, 71
  visits, 219 pageviews — and every one of them falls on 09-04, 09-05 or 09-06.
  Its "last 30 days" label was the API's period name, not the span of data.
- **The headline Δ below is mostly window length, not growth.** 55 visitors in
  3 days is 18 a day; 101 in 8 days is 13 a day. Measured daily, the audience is
  **decaying from the 09-05 launch spike**, not growing.

Because v4.6.0 reached production on **2026-09-07 at 20:27 UTC**, the 8 days
split cleanly into two 4-day halves, and that comparison carries most of this
report:

- **Before** — 2026-09-04 → 2026-09-07 (v4.5.0 live)
- **After** — 2026-09-08 → 2026-09-11 (v4.6.0 live: the fiche « Poursuivre »
  chapter, navigable associated peoples, the widened Mercator round bank, and the
  first interaction events)

v4.7.0 and v4.8.0 shipped on 2026-09-12, after the window. The next audit
measures them; nothing here does.

## Findings

1. The measured window is 8 days (collection began 2026-09-04), and the previous baseline was 3 days, not 30; daily visitors fell from 35 on 09-05 to 4 on 09-11.
2. After v4.6.0 site bounce fell from 55 % to 34 %, and Google arrivals on peuple fiches bounced 4 times in 13 against 11 in 13 before — the four dead-end fiches of the last report did not recur.
3. `fiche:related_click` recorded 0 events after v4.6.0 on a page type where bounce fell 16 points, so either readers continued through something other than the « Poursuivre » chapter or the event does not fire in production — this report cannot credit the chapter.
4. `/fr/jeux/mercator` remains the site's worst page over the window (20 visitors, 81 % bounce, 69 % scroll), 15 of its 20 visitors came from LinkedIn, and 0 of 3 players completed a game.
5. v4.6.0 changed Mercator's rounds, not its ending; its after-release bounce of 33 % rests on 4 visitors and does not yet evidence a fix.
6. LinkedIn fell from 29 visitors to 5 between the two halves; Google held at 16 → 15 while its bounce fell from 82 % to 27 %, making it the steady channel.
7. Search Console is not connected, so the queries behind 31 Google visitors — 26 of whose entries landed on peuple fiches — are unknown.
8. UTM tagging is live since 2026-09-09 and has recorded one scheme-tagged visit: the YouTube `traore-diop` click, which bounced from `PAT_TRAORE` in 2 seconds at 18 % scroll.
9. The mobile–desktop bounce gap closed after v4.6.0 (33 % vs 36 %), but mobile visits still last a quarter as long (1m22 vs 5m04).
10. Readership halves at each quartile of a fiche: `fiche:depth` counts 18 visitors at 25 %, 9 at 50 %, 6 at 75 %, 3 at 100 %.
11. 16 visitors reached `/fr/atlas/recherche` with a 0 % bounce but only 2 fired `search:submit`, and `search:result_click` is emitted but not registered as a goal, so how search hands readers onward is unmeasured.
12. 44 of 101 visitors are in African countries; Côte d'Ivoire (15) is the second country after France (45).
13. Fiche coverage rose from 45 to 82 URLs (4.9 % of 1 684), driven by peuple fiches (21 → 49); patronymes stay at 1.5 %.

## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- **Priority 1 — Mercator's ending, still unbuilt.** Findings 4 and 5. v4.6.0
  varied the rounds; nothing yet gives a finished or abandoned session a next
  step, and no player has reached `game:complete`. Two leaks to design for, in
  order: half of the after-release visitors never started a game (2 of 4), and
  none of the starters finished. The audience is LinkedIn-on-mobile — design at
  430 px. Falsifier: Mercator bounce and `game:complete / game:start` in the next
  audit, at no fewer than 15 visitors after the change.
- **Priority 2 — prove or disprove the « Poursuivre » chapter before building on
  it.** Finding 3. On production, open a peuple fiche, click a « Poursuivre » link
  and confirm a `fiche:related_click` reaches Plausible. If it arrives, the chapter
  is not what lowered bounce and the continuation lives elsewhere (header, search,
  breadcrumb) — find which. If it does not, fix the event; the 85 % → 31 % of
  finding 2 cannot be attributed until it fires. Do not redesign the peuple fiche
  exit in the meantime: the measured direction is good.
- **Priority 3 — the `traore-diop` landing.** Finding 8. The one tagged video
  click left `PAT_TRAORE` in 2 seconds at 18 % scroll. n=1: check, do not
  redesign. Open the page at 430 px and ask whether its first screen keeps the
  promise the Traoré/Diop video made. A landing that fails that test spends every
  click the campaign earns.
- **Priority 4 — the mobile duration gap.** Finding 9. Mobile bounce is fixed;
  mobile visits still end at a quarter of desktop's length. Combine with finding
  10 — where on a 430 px fiche does the 50 % → 25 % halving happen?
- **Instrumentation, not design — hand back rather than designing around it:**
  - Register `search:result_click` as a Plausible goal (finding 11). Goals are
    applied when queried, so events already recorded should appear once it exists.
  - `fiche:source_click` is declared and emitted nowhere — the atlas's own
    promise (a source consulted) is unmeasured.
  - `page:not_found` did not fire on a 404 pageview (`/fr/dossier`). Check
    whether the path renders the `[lang]` not-found boundary or the root one.

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- **Do not read a cadence off this report.** Finding 1. It holds 8 days of data,
  half of them a launch spike. The first report with a full 30 days of collection
  is possible from 2026-10-04; until then, compare halves, not months.
- **Google is the channel that holds, and it runs on peuple fiches.** Findings 6,
  7 and 13. It needs no publication, it doubled peuple discovery, and after
  v4.6.0 it bounces least of any source (27 %, tied with Direct) — though its
  visits are short, 1m05 against Direct's 5m58. Connecting Search Console to the
  site is the precondition to choosing subjects from demand rather than taste —
  bring the query list before proposing the next peuple-anchored subject.
- **LinkedIn is a spike, not a baseline.** Finding 6. 29 → 5 visitors between
  two consecutive 4-day halves. Before reweighting the plan toward it, line its
  visitor days up against the actual LinkedIn post dates: if the spikes follow
  posts, cadence is the lever; if not, the launch was.
- **The video channels still cannot be judged.** Finding 8. Tagging is three
  days old and has produced one tagged visit. Do not cut or scale TikTok,
  Instagram, YouTube or Facebook on this report. Check that every published
  description uses the link-builder's links — the Instagram visit carried `ig`,
  not the scheme's `instagram`.
- **Mercator is still the audience's door from LinkedIn.** Finding 4. The demand
  for the true-size-of-Africa subject is proven again (15 LinkedIn arrivals),
  but sending more readers to a page none of them has finished spends them.
  Wait for the optimizer's Priority 1 before routing a campaign there.
- **A measured West and Central African readership exists.** Finding 12.
  Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, the two Congos, Benin and Senegal account for 33
  visitors. It is a sample of consented sessions, not a market study, but it is
  the first geographic evidence the plan can cite.
- **Patronymes remain the largest undiscovered asset.** Finding 13. 796 fiches at
  1.5 % coverage, unchanged in rank since the last report.

## Method

Every figure comes from the instance's unauthenticated internal JSON API
(`/api/stats/ethniafrica.com/…`) with `detailed=true` and explicit limits:
`top-stats`, `pages`, `entry-pages`, `exit-pages`, `sources`, `screen-sizes`,
`countries`, `conversions`, `utm_sources`, `utm_mediums`, `utm_campaigns`,
`referrers/{source}`, `custom-prop-values/{prop}` filtered by goal, and
`main-graph` for the daily curve. The before/after halves are
`period=custom&from=…&to=…` queries; the previous window was re-queried the same
way and reproduced the 2026-09-07 figures exactly. Configured goals come from
`suggestions/goal`.

Per-source entry pages use `filters=[["is","visit:source",["<source>"]]]`. The
visitor-weighted bounce, scroll and time for peuple fiches are computed from the
`pages` rows of each half; rows with no scroll or time value are left out of that
average.

Release times from `gh release list`; which release carried which change from
`git tag --contains`. Corpus counts from
`find dataset/source/afrik -name '*.json'`. The `/fr/dossier` status was read
from production with a single request.

**For the next run:** the `Reference baseline — 2026-09-07 (30 days)` section
of this skill's `SKILL.md` describes a 3-day window. Read `main-graph` before
labelling any period.
