# Dossier illustrations — provenance and licences

Each image is evidence used by its chapter: a projection whose geometry is
discussed, a population pyramid whose shape is measured, or the mine at the
start of the cobalt value chain. Licence metadata was read from the Wikimedia
Commons API on 2026-09-06. The visible dossier captions repeat the author,
licence, licence URL, and file-page URL required for attribution.

All three files use Wikimedia Commons' 1920 px rendition. No crop or colour
edit was applied.

## `equal-earth-projection.jpg`

- **Work**: Equal Earth projection on a derivative of NASA's Blue Marble
  summer composite, with a 15-degree graticule (2018).
- **Author**: Strebe.
- **Source**: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Equal_Earth_projection_SW.jpg)
- **Licence**: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Why this one**: it is the projection named by the UN resolution and
  discussed by the chapter.

## `africa-population-pyramid-2023.png`

- **Work**: Population pyramid of Africa in 2023, built from the UN World
  Population Prospects data portal.
- **Author**: Tweedle.
- **Source**: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Africa_population_pyramid_2023.svg)
- **Licence**: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Why this one**: the chapter examines both the measured age structure and
  the political language placed around that structure.

## `mutanda-mine-satellite.jpg`

- **Work**: CBERS-4 false-colour satellite image of the open-pit Mutanda
  copper and cobalt mine near Kolwezi, Democratic Republic of the Congo
  (image dated 2018-03-09).
- **Author**: Coordenação-Geral de Observação da Terra / INPE.
- **Source**: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mina_de_Mutanda_na_Rep%C3%BAblica_Democr%C3%A1tica_do_Congo.jpg)
- **Licence**: [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).
- **Why this one**: it shows the extraction site at the first stage of the
  value chain that the chapter follows through refining and manufacturing.
